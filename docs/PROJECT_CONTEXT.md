## Product direction

Use a premium outdoor aesthetic: forest green, dark teal, warm off-white, sand and restrained campfire amber. Prioritize images, clear information, simple navigation and direct contact. No fake testimonials, unsupported accreditation, invented statistics, destination names, prices, event dates, policies or personnel.

Primary journey: Instagram/Google → homepage → adventure details → understand location/date/price/program/availability → contact → future confirmed reservation workflow.
